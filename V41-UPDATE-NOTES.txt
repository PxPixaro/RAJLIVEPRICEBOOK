V41 VOICE NATURAL PHRASE SEARCH
- Voice phrases such as "Bharat Benz Gadi" now reduce to meaningful Excel search terms such as BHARATBENZ / BHARAT BENZ.
- Generic spoken filler words such as gadi/gaadi/vehicle/model/group/brand are ignored when useful.
- User-defined VOICE ALIASES remain supported and take priority.
- Global voice search is not trapped by programmatic/default group selections.
- Only filters manually selected/typed by the customer create a scoped universal-search context.
- With no manual scope, clear group/segment/vehicle/model names may auto-select the matching filter; otherwise search runs across all Excel rows/columns.
- V40 performance, V38 PDF format, watermark and all-group PDF logic retained.
